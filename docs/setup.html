Step 3 — Your GoMining account(s)

You'll grab your GoMining login with a one-click bookmark. No DevTools, no copying values by hand.

  1. One time only: drag this button up to your bookmarks bar → 📋 Capture GoMining Cookies
  2. Open a private / incognito window and log into app.gomining.com. Why private? GoMining changes your login every time it's used, so if the automation and your everyday browser share one login they can knock each other out. Afterwards just close the window — don't click "Log out".
  3. Click the bookmark. A green Copied! note appears on the GoMining page.
  4. Come back to this tab. Your login fills in below by itself — or press Paste from clipboard (the first time, your browser asks permission to read the clipboard; allow it).

No GitHub token needed. The wizard makes a random encryption key for you and stores it in your repo's Secrets; your automation uses it to keep each refreshed login safely in GitHub's cache.
