Step 3 — Your GoMining account(s)

You'll grab your GoMining login with a one-click bookmark. No DevTools, no copying values by hand.

  1. One time only: drag this button up to your bookmarks bar → 📋 Capture GoMining Cookies
  2. Open a private / incognito window and log into app.gomining.com. Why private? GoMining changes your login every time it's used, so if the automation and your everyday browser share one login they can knock each other out. Afterwards just close the window — don't click "Log out".
  3. Click the bookmark. A green Copied! note appears on the GoMining page.
  4. Come back to this tab. Your login fills in below by itself — or press Paste from clipboard (the first time, your browser asks permission to read the clipboard; allow it).

Self-refresh token (required)

Because GoMining changes your login each time it's used, your automation has to save the new one back to your repo after every run. Without this token it works about once and then stops. GitHub doesn't let a website create this token for you, but the form can be mostly pre-filled — three quick steps:

  1. Open the token form → (opens in a new tab; the name, permission and expiry are already filled in)
  2. Scroll to Repository access. The form starts on "All repositories" — change it to "Only select repositories" and pick (the token should only ever be able to touch that one repo)
  3. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it here:

This token lasts a year — worth a calendar reminder, since the automation stops saving your login when it expires.
